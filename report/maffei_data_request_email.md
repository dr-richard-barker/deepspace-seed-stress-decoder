Dear Prof. Maffei,

I am carrying out a meta-analysis of Arabidopsis transcriptional responses to reduced/near-null
magnetic fields and am building on two of your group's microarray studies:
1. *"Transcriptomics and Metabolomics of Reactive Oxygen Species Modulation in Near-Null Magnetic
   Field-Induced Arabidopsis thaliana"* (Biomolecules 2022, 12, 1824; doi:10.3390/biom12121824), and
2. *"Differential root and shoot magnetoresponses in Arabidopsis thaliana"* (Sci. Rep. 2021, 11, 9195;
   doi:10.1038/s41598-021-88695-6) — the magnetic-field dose-response (240 nT/40 nT near-null through
   ~60 µT).

The published supplements provide curated DEG subsets, which have been extremely useful. For the
meta-analysis I would like to include the **full genome-wide microarray results** from both studies
(all probes, all root/shoot timepoints and field strengths). Both papers state data are available as
supplementary tables and further data upon request, and I was unable to locate a GEO/ArrayExpress deposit.

Would you be willing to share either (a) the raw array files (e.g., Agilent .txt/.gpr), or (b) the
processed per-probe expression / log-ratio matrix with the sample design? I am glad to cite the dataset
per your preference and to acknowledge the contribution; I am also happy to deposit a harmonized
version in a public repository on your behalf if that would be helpful.

Thank you very much for considering this — and for the original study.
